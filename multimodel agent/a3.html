AI-DRIVEN CLINICAL SPEECH PROCESSING FOR EARLY ALZHEIMER'S SCREENING

A Multimodal Deep Learning Framework via Gated Cross-Modal Attention

Authors: Dr. K. Jayanthi, Harish R | Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology

Introduction & Objective

  • The Problem: Early detection of Alzheimer’s disease (AD) relies on invasive and costly neuroimaging. By the time clinical symptoms appear, irreversible damage has occurred.
  • The Solution: Speech acts as a non-invasive digital biomarker. AI can detect subtle acoustic and linguistic anomalies years before memory loss.
  • Objective: To build a scalable machine learning framework that detects Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI) using raw clinical audio recordings.

System Methodology

  • Data Acquisition: Spontaneous speech samples (e.g., picture description tasks) from clinical datasets.
  • Speech Denoising & ASR: Advanced filtering isolates voice; Automated Speech Recognition transcribes audio while preserving hesitations and pauses.
  • Dual-Stream Modeling: Utilizing WavLM for acoustic embeddings and ClinicalBERT for domain-specific linguistic tokens.

Key Features & Biomarkers

Acoustic Biomarkers:

  • Reduced prosody (pitch variability).
  • Increased pause lengths and slow speech rate.
  • Changes in phonation and articulation.

Linguistic Biomarkers:

  • Reduced vocabulary and lexical diversity.
  • Syntactic simplification.
  • Lower pronoun-to-noun ratios.

Performance & Accuracy

  • High Precision: Cross-modal attention effectively captures the correlation between *what* is said and *how* it is spoken.
  • Accuracy: Multimodal systems routinely outperform single-modality approaches, achieving up to 89-93% diagnostic accuracy.
  • Early Detection: Identifies MCI markers up to 6 years prior to clinical AD onset.

Conclusion & Future Work

The integration of WavLM and ClinicalBERT through a PyTorch Conformer represents a highly effective, low-cost screening tool.

Future Applications: Deployment of this AI framework as a remote diagnostic application, enabling frequent, non-invasive cognitive assessments in standard clinical workflows.
